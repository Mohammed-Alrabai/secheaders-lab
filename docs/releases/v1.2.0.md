# SecHeaders Lab v1.2.0

**From a report to a plan.** The results page now tells you what to fix first and how much it helps. Scores and grades are unchanged.

## Added

- **Improve your grade.** The top three fixes, chosen by how much they raise your grade, each with its projected score. A what-if selector shows the projected score for any combination of fixes, and every missing header is gathered into one copy-ready block for Nginx, Apache, IIS, Next.js or Netlify. Projections use the same scoring model as the report.
- **Compare with earlier scans.** Turn on a private history kept only in your browser (off by default, up to 20 scans) to see how a site's score, grade and individual checks change over time. Turning it off deletes it. The Privacy and Cookie Policies describe it.
- **Certificate key details.** The TLS panel shows the certificate's key type and size and notes keys smaller than commonly recommended. Informational only.
- **Threat model.** A STRIDE threat model of the tool itself, with data-flow diagrams and the controls behind every mitigation.
- **Daily self-scan.** A scheduled workflow scans the live site every day and opens an issue if it drops below A+ / 100.
